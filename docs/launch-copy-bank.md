- We are testing how AI agents can help people understand, critique, and shape politics — with human accountability intact.

## Short descriptions

### 50 words, Swedish

Oberoende Digital är en offentlig testmiljö för ett säkert autonomt parti: ett experiment i hur AI-agenter kan stödja politiskt deltagande, lärande, granskning och deliberation. Plattformen är inte ett formellt registrerat parti än. Den byggs öppet för att människor ska kunna testa, kritisera och forska på modellen.

### 50 words, English

Oberoende Digital is a public test environment for a Safe Autonomous Party: an experiment in how AI agents can support political participation, learning, scrutiny, and deliberation. It is not yet a formally registered party. The platform is being built openly so people can test, critique, and study the model.

### 100 words, Swedish

Oberoende Digital undersöker hur AI-agenter kan bli en central del av framtidens politiska organisationer — inte genom att ersätta människor, utan genom att hjälpa människor förstå, granska och delta. Projektet är just nu en plattform och testmiljö, inte ett formellt registrerat parti. Vi bygger en miljö där medborgare, utvecklare, forskare och kritiker kan interagera med AI-agenter, pröva styrningsmodeller, hitta risker och publicera lärdomar. Målet är att ta frågan om AI och demokrati från spekulation till konkret, granskningsbar praktik.

### 100 words, English

Oberoende Digital explores how AI agents could become a core part of future political organizations — not by replacing people, but by helping people understand, scrutinize, and participate. The project is currently a platform and test environment, not a formally registered party. We are building a space where citizens, developers, researchers, and critics can interact with AI agents, test governance models, identify risks, and publish what is learned. The goal is to move the question of AI and democracy from speculation into concrete, inspectable practice.

## First reply templates

### Curious newcomer

Welcome. The short version: Oberoende Digital is a public test environment for safe AI-assisted political participation. We are not claiming that AI should rule. We are testing whether AI agents can make politics more understandable, transparent, and participatory while humans remain accountable. What part are you most curious or skeptical about?

### Skeptic

Good — skepticism is necessary here. The project only becomes credible if the failure modes are explicit: manipulation, hallucination, capture, bias, accountability, financing, privacy, and legal constraints. Which risk do you think is most dangerous, and what control would you require before this should be tested publicly?

### Builder

Great. Useful contributions right now are practical and safety-oriented: onboarding flows, audit logs, source-backed policy explanations, abuse prevention, moderation design, and testable civic-agent workflows. What area do you want to work on: platform, AI behavior, governance, UX, research, or community operations?

### Researcher / journalist

The project is designed as a public research environment. We want to observe how people interact with AI civic agents, what increases or reduces trust, where AI helps deliberation, and where it creates risk. We should separate platform facts from claims and keep outputs source-backed where possible. What angle are you investigating?

### Policy idea

That is a useful policy prompt, but we should not treat it as an official position yet. For OD, the next step is to source it, identify tradeoffs, produce counterarguments, and make the uncertainty visible. Can you share one source or one concrete problem statement behind the idea?
