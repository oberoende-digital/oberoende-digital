# Oberoende Digital Discord onboarding structure

Public invite: https://discord.gg/4A7AcwVGy3

## Purpose

Welcome to Independent Digital. This server is a space for learning about the project, discussing policy, testing ideas, and helping build the platform.

Independent Digital is a policy experiment exploring how AI-supported political participation, transparency, and platform-based governance could work in practice.

Important: Independent Digital is currently a policy experiment and debate platform, not a registered political party.

## Onboarding question

Use Discord Community Onboarding with multiple choices allowed.

Question:

What would you like to do in Independent Digital?

Answer options:

- 📰 I just want general information and updates
- 🤖 I want to talk to OD’s AI
- 🛠️ I want to help develop the platform
- 🏛️ I want to influence OD politically
- 🔎 I want to review, critique, or stress-test the idea

Role mapping:

- General information → @Info & AI
- Talk to OD’s AI → @Info & AI
- Develop the platform → @Developer Applicant first; promote manually to @Platform Developer
- Influence politically → @Political Participation
- Review / critique → @Reviewer

## Suggested permission model

- @Visitor: read START HERE and limited GENERAL only.
- @Info & AI: access announcements, general info, talk-to-od-ai, questions-and-answers.
- @Political Participation: access politics and policy channels.
- @Developer Applicant: access dev-start and public development intake, not sensitive systems.
- @Platform Developer: access platform development channels after manual approval.
- @Reviewer: access scrutiny-and-critique, sources-and-background, security-and-risk read or limited write depending on trust.
- @OD AI: post in talk-to-od-ai and ai-test-channel; do not grant moderation/admin privileges.
- @Moderator: manage messages, incidents, moderator-log.
- @Admin: full server administration.
