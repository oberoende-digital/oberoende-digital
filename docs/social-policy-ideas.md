# Social-first policy suggestion backlog

Purpose: maintain a shortlist pipeline for headline-friendly ideas that can be investigated, sourced, costed, and turned into social posts. These are prompts for research and deliberation, not final party positions.

## Shortlist candidates from the first brainstorm

1. Free Swedish Civic Cloud: secure basic storage for every resident, operated as public digital infrastructure through the National Library or another trusted public institution.
2. Free AI-assisted CBT/KBT: clinically supervised AI support as a first-line mental-health tool, with human escalation and public evaluation.
3. School profit dividend: if independent schools generate surplus while meeting verified quality and equity targets, part of the surplus is shared with students or reinvested locally.
4. A national wellbeing index: Sweden should publish and steer by mental health, trust, safety, future confidence and quality-of-life indicators alongside GDP.
5. Personal AI ombudsman for every citizen: a public AI assistant that helps people understand rights, benefits, forms and decisions.
6. A Swedish AI safety institute with public red-team reports: test public-sector AI before deployment and publish failures.
7. Open budget simulator for citizens: every budget proposal must be explorable in plain language with tradeoffs visible.
8. Algorithmic transparency label: every public algorithm gets a simple label: purpose, data, human appeal route, and audit date.
9. National loneliness guarantee: anyone who asks for help should be connected to local social, voluntary, clinical or digital support within days.
10. Public-interest social media feeds: publicly funded open recommendation algorithms for news, democracy and learning.
11. AI tutors for all children: free curriculum-aligned tutoring, with teacher control and privacy by design.
12. A youth future account: every young person gets a public learning/transition budget usable for education, therapy, mentorship or career change.
13. Swedish data dividend: when public data creates commercial AI value, part of the upside funds public digital infrastructure.
14. Right to a human decision: AI may help the state, but citizens can always require a human appeal path.
15. A national attention tax debate: explore fees on manipulative engagement design, earmarked for mental health and media literacy.
16. Open-source procurement first: public software should default to open standards and open source unless there is a documented reason not to.
17. Digital civil defense for households: every resident gets practical support for backups, scams, privacy, AI fraud and crisis communication.
18. Climate consequence receipts: major policy proposals should show climate, health, inequality and budget effects in one public receipt.
19. Fast-track evidence reviews: a standing AI-assisted evidence office that answers political claims in days, not months.
20. Public compute for researchers and civic builders: national GPU/AI credits for open, audited public-interest projects.
21. The 4-day-workweek evidence trial: large public pilots with wellbeing, productivity and staffing metrics published transparently.
22. Zero-bureaucracy benefits: state agencies should proactively suggest benefits and support a citizen may be entitled to.
23. AI-assisted eldercare admin: reduce paperwork for staff, not human contact for the elderly.
24. A Swedish right to repair guarantee: products bought with public money must be repairable and documented.
25. National food resilience dashboard: transparent tracking of critical food supply, imports, prices and vulnerabilities.
26. Public deliberation juries: randomly selected citizens review major reforms with AI evidence support and publish minority reports.
27. Student loan pause for public-interest work: defer repayments for verified civic, care, climate or open-source contributions.
28. Mental health checkups as normal as dental care: opt-in yearly checkups with strong privacy protections.
29. Scam-loss rapid response: a public-private emergency path for fraud victims before money disappears across accounts.
30. AI-generated law summaries: every new law must have a plain-language, source-linked explanation and impact summary.
31. No dark patterns in public services: public digital services should be the gold standard for clarity, accessibility and consent.
32. Personal carbon-and-cost planner: voluntary tools that help households cut bills and emissions without moralizing.
33. Public service AI voice for accessibility: citizens can talk to public services in plain Swedish, minority languages and accessible formats.
34. Transparent waiting-list map: healthcare queues visible by region, treatment type and referral route.
35. Anti-corruption open meetings ledger: easy search over meetings between elected officials, lobbyists and major interest groups.
36. National resilience service year: voluntary paid year in care, civil defense, climate adaptation, tutoring or digital defense.
37. Library of Things expansion: municipalities lend tools, devices, sensors and repair kits, not only books.
38. Child online-safety default: device/platform settings optimized for wellbeing, sleep and consent for minors.
39. Public AI incident register: failures, near misses and corrections from public-sector AI are logged openly.
40. Citizen-source policy challenges: people submit problems; OD publishes evidence maps, tradeoffs and proposed experiments.

## Social post format

Each shortlisted idea should get:

- one 5–8 word headline in Swedish
- one concrete promise
- one safety/feasibility caveat
- one source link to `https://oberoendedigital.se`
- one QR code back to the homepage or a source page
- image prompt in OD visual style

## Image/QR production workflow

1. Shortlist 3–5 ideas.
2. For each: write Swedish headline, subline, source/caveat.
3. Generate square and portrait variants.
4. Add QR code pointing to `https://oberoendedigital.se` or a specific policy source page.
5. Publish only after the claim has a source note and an internal caveat.
