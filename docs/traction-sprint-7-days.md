# Oberoende Digital: 7-day traction sprint

Purpose: create visible public momentum for Oberoende Digital without overclaiming formal party status. The public hook is bold; the operating layer stays serious.

Primary CTA for the sprint: join the Discord community.

- Website: https://oberoendedigital.se/
- Discord: https://discord.gg/4A7AcwVGy3
- Core framing: Världens första säkra autonoma parti.
- English framing: The world’s first Safe Autonomous Party.

## Guardrails

Use this wording consistently:

- Oberoende Digital is a platform and public test environment for AI-assisted democratic participation.
- It is not yet a formally registered political party.
- AI agents can help structure, summarize, critique, and prototype political participation, but humans remain accountable.
- Public interaction should invite participation, questions, critique, and research collaboration.

Do not publish:

- claims that OD is already a registered party
- internal formation timelines
- private white-paper links unless explicitly approved
- donation or membership claims before the legal/operational path is ready
- policy promises as final party positions unless sourced and reviewed

## Day 2 — Make the safety distinction

Goal: prevent “AI party” from sounding reckless.

Post:

> “Autonomous” does not mean unaccountable.
>
> For Oberoende Digital, autonomy must be bounded by:
> - human responsibility
> - audit logs
> - source transparency
> - red-team testing
> - appeal paths
> - public research
>
> The question is not “should AI rule?”
> The question is: can AI make politics more understandable, inspectable, and participatory?
>
> https://oberoendedigital.se/

Discord prompt:

> What is the strongest argument against an AI-agent-centered political platform?
>
> Post it here. We want the critique first.

## Metrics to track daily

Minimum dashboard:

- website visits
- Discord joins
- Discord messages
- number of unique participants
- number of serious critiques/questions
- number of contributor leads
- number of research/press leads
- posts published
- replies/DMs sent

Qualitative signal:

- Are people asking “is this real?”
- Are skeptics engaging seriously?
- Are builders offering help?
- Are researchers asking about data/methodology?
- Are people confused about whether this is a formal party? If yes, tighten disclaimers.

## Response posture in Discord

The bot should respond when people write, but with this stance:

- welcome questions
- ask clarifying questions
- separate speculation from current project facts
- invite critique
- avoid declaring final policy positions
- route people to channels: public questions, policy ideas, platform development, safety/risk
- remind users that OD is currently a platform/test environment, not a formally registered party

Short Discord welcome response:

> Welcome to Oberoende Digital. This is a public experiment in safe AI-assisted political participation. The best contribution right now is a sharp question, a critique, or a concrete use case. What do you want this platform to prove — or fail to prove?
