# Privacy Policy, GDPR Notice and Cookie Notice

Version: 2026-06-02

Public PDF path: `/privacy-gdpr.pdf`

This document is a practical privacy and GDPR notice for Oberoende Digital's public website and early community/research platform. It is based on the transparency and rights structure used in GDPR notices and on public guidance from the European Commission, the European Data Protection Board (EDPB) and the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY). It is not a substitute for review by a qualified lawyer.

Reference guidance used as a base:

- European Commission — Information for individuals / GDPR rights: https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/reform/rights-citizens/my-rights_en
- European Data Protection Board — Guidelines 05/2020 on consent under Regulation 2016/679: https://www.edpb.europa.eu/our-work-tools/our-documents/guidelines/guidelines-052020-consent-under-regulation-2016679_en
- Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY) — data protection for organisations and individuals: https://www.imy.se/en/organisations/data-protection/ and https://www.imy.se/en/individuals/data-protection/
- Swedish Post and Telecom Authority (PTS) — cookies information: https://www.pts.se/en/private/internet/cookies/

## 1. Who we are

Oberoende Digital is a pre-formal platform build and research environment exploring AI-assisted democratic deliberation, policy formation, transparency and governance. The project is not yet a formally registered political party.

Data controller / contact point: Oberoende Digital

Contact email: admin@oberoendedigital.se

Website: https://oberoendedigital.se

Important status note: because the project is still pre-formal, the legal entity, postal address, organisation number and formal Data Protection Officer details may need to be updated before processing sensitive member data, donations, voting records or political participation records at scale.

## 2. What this notice covers

This notice covers personal data processed through:

- the public website at `oberoendedigital.se`;
- the interest registration form;
- public community links such as Discord;
- early research, feedback and participation workflows connected to the Safe Autonomous Party Lab;
- basic technical logs generated by hosting, form handling and security systems.

Separate notices may be needed later for formal membership, verified voting, donations, employment/volunteer administration, research studies, AI-agent logs or moderation workflows.

## 3. Personal data we may process

Depending on how you interact with us, we may process:

- email address submitted through the interest form;
- name, handle or contact details if you voluntarily provide them;
- message content, questions, proposals or feedback you send to us;
- Discord username, account identifier and public server messages if you interact in the Discord community;
- technical data such as IP address, browser type, device information, timestamps, referring pages and basic server/security logs;
- cookie or local browser preference data, such as language preference and cookie-banner acknowledgement;
- consent records and communication preferences.

We do not intentionally collect special-category political opinions, health data, biometric data, national identity numbers or formal voting records through the public homepage. If future platform features require those categories, they must have a separate privacy notice, lawful basis, security model and retention policy before launch.

## 4. Why we process data and our lawful bases

We process personal data only when we have a lawful basis under the GDPR.

### Interest registration and updates

Purpose: to receive your email address and contact you about the platform opening, participation opportunities or registration start.

Lawful basis: consent or legitimate interest, depending on the specific interaction. You can withdraw consent or ask us to stop contacting you at any time.

### Community participation and Discord

Purpose: to allow discussion, answer questions, moderate the community, investigate abuse and improve the public project.

Lawful basis: legitimate interest in operating a safe public community, and consent where you voluntarily choose to join or submit information.

### Research and feedback

Purpose: to understand questions, concerns, risks, participation patterns and governance needs related to AI-assisted democratic systems.

Lawful basis: legitimate interest in research and platform improvement, consent where requested, and additional safeguards if future formal studies are conducted.

### Security, abuse prevention and operations

Purpose: to prevent spam, fraud, misuse, harassment, unauthorised access, service disruption and data loss.

Lawful basis: legitimate interest and, where applicable, legal obligation.

### Cookies and local preferences

Purpose: to remember language and cookie notice choices and operate the website.

Lawful basis: strictly necessary storage for site functionality; consent if non-essential analytics, advertising or tracking cookies are added later.

## 5. Cookies and local browser storage

The current public website is designed to use minimal storage.

The site may store:

- language preference, so the site can remember Swedish or English;
- cookie-banner acknowledgement, so the banner does not reappear every time;
- basic technical data processed by the browser and hosting provider.

At the time this document was prepared, the site did not intentionally use advertising cookies, behavioural tracking cookies or analytics cookies. The site loads external Google Fonts and links to third-party services such as Discord and FormSubmit. Those third parties may process data according to their own policies when your browser connects to them or when you click/use their services.

If Oberoende Digital later adds analytics, embedded media, pixels, advertising, heatmaps or other non-essential tracking, the cookie banner and this notice must be updated before those tools are enabled.

## 6. Third-party services

We may use or link to third-party services, including:

- GitHub Pages for static website hosting and source publication;
- FormSubmit for forwarding interest-form submissions by email;
- Discord for community discussion;
- Google Fonts for web typography;
- GitHub for code, documents, issue tracking and public transparency.

When you use these services, their own privacy policies may also apply. We try to keep the public website simple, transparent and low-tracking, but third-party providers may process technical data such as IP address, user-agent and timestamps.

## 7. Sharing of data

We do not sell personal data.

We may share or make data accessible to:

- service providers that host, secure, transmit or process website/community data;
- project maintainers and authorised operators who need access to run the project;
- public GitHub repositories or Discord channels, if you choose to post information publicly;
- authorities or legal advisers when required by law or necessary to protect rights, safety or security.

## 8. International transfers

Some service providers may process data outside Sweden or the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Where GDPR transfer rules apply, transfers should rely on appropriate safeguards such as adequacy decisions, standard contractual clauses or provider-specific transfer mechanisms.

Because the project is still early-stage, formal transfer assessments should be completed before sensitive participation, membership, donation, voting or political-opinion processing is launched.

## 9. Retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes described above.

Indicative retention approach:

- interest-form emails: until you unsubscribe, ask for deletion, or the purpose is no longer active;
- Discord/community messages: according to Discord settings and moderation needs;
- security logs: for a limited operational period unless needed to investigate abuse or incidents;
- research notes: anonymised or aggregated where possible;
- consent and suppression records: as long as needed to respect your choices and document compliance.

A more detailed retention schedule should be adopted before formal membership or voting features are launched.

## 10. Your GDPR rights

Subject to GDPR conditions and exceptions, you have the right to:

- access your personal data;
- correct inaccurate data;
- request deletion;
- restrict processing;
- object to processing based on legitimate interests;
- withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent;
- request data portability where applicable;
- lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

In Sweden, the supervisory authority is Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten (IMY): https://www.imy.se/

To exercise your rights, contact: admin@oberoendedigital.se

## 11. AI-assisted processing

Oberoende Digital is explicitly exploring AI-assisted democratic systems. Public AI tools may help summarize, draft, classify, answer questions, detect abuse, organize feedback or support research.

Current principle: AI may assist, summarize, analyze, draft, critique and audit. Democratic legitimacy remains human, auditable, contestable and transparent.

If AI tools are used to process personal data, Oberoende Digital should apply privacy-by-design safeguards, including minimisation, human oversight, logging, access control, source transparency, correction mechanisms and clear limits on automated decision-making.

No solely automated decision with legal or similarly significant effect should be made about individuals through the public homepage.

## 12. Security

We use proportionate technical and organisational measures for the current stage of the project. These may include access controls, least-privilege operator access, repository review, secure handling of credentials, moderation processes and audit trails.

Security measures must be strengthened before the project processes sensitive categories of data, formal membership records, donations, identity verification, voting records or high-risk AI-agent logs.

## 13. Children's data

The public site is not directed at children. If future youth participation is enabled, the project must adopt age-appropriate privacy information, consent/guardian rules where required and additional safeguards.

## 14. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice as the platform, legal status and processing activities develop. The current version date appears at the top of the document.

## 15. Svensk sammanfattning

Oberoende Digital samlar i nuläget in så lite persondata som möjligt. Den viktigaste informationen är e-postadress om du själv anmäler intresse, meddelanden eller frågor du skickar, tekniska driftloggar samt begränsade lokala webbläsarinställningar som språkval och cookie-besked.

Vi säljer inte persondata. Vi använder data för att kunna driva webbplatsen, svara på frågor, hantera intresseanmälningar, moderera communityt, förbättra labbet och skydda tjänsten mot missbruk.

Du kan kontakta admin@oberoendedigital.se för att begära tillgång, rättelse, radering, begränsning, invändning eller återkallelse av samtycke. Du kan också lämna klagomål till Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten, IMY.

Viktig begränsning: projektet är fortfarande en för-formell plattform och forskningsmiljö. Innan formellt medlemskap, verifierad röstning, donationer eller behandling av politiska åsikter sker i större skala bör detta dokument granskas juridiskt och kompletteras med organisationsnummer, postadress, tydlig personuppgiftsansvarig, retentionsschema, personuppgiftsbiträdesavtal och eventuella konsekvensbedömningar.
