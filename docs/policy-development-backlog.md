# OD policy development backlog

Status: draft public/member backlog. This is not a locked programme, not a binding mandate, and not a claim that OD has already adopted final positions. It is a transparent queue for evidence-first policy work derived from the OD White Paper v2.2 and the Hermes Backend Instruction v2.

## Operating rules

- **Human legitimacy:** AI systems may draft, compare evidence, and expose trade-offs, but final political decisions require human members and the documented OD decision process.
- **Advisory polls only in MVP:** Polls and AI-agent vote signals are advisory until a human-admin gate, a Mandate Registry, and quantified thresholds explicitly upgrade a decision path.
- **Transparent AI identity:** Any AI-authored or AI-assisted policy work must be labeled as such, with uncertainty and source limits visible.
- **Evidence before position:** Every backlog item needs sources, assumptions, affected groups, uncertainty, and a correction path before it can become a public position.
- **Privacy by design:** No political-profile, voting, or research personal data may be collected for these items before DPIA/ROPA/data-governance gates are complete.
- **No autonomous spending:** Research purchases, provider integrations, ads, crypto, or campaign spending remain disabled unless a human accountable owner approves them.

## Evidence-first policy template

Each policy backlog item should produce a small public issue or document using this template:

1. **Question:** What exact policy question is being examined?
2. **Why now:** What White Paper goal, public concern, or operational risk makes this relevant?
3. **Current Swedish/EU baseline:** What rules, statistics, institutions, and public commitments already exist?
4. **Evidence map:** Sources for benefits, harms, costs, uncertainty, and counterarguments.
5. **Stakeholders:** Who benefits, who bears risk, and which groups may be underrepresented?
6. **System effects:** 10–50-year effects, feedback loops, trade-offs, and unintended consequences.
7. **Data and rights review:** GDPR/sensitive-political-data implications, equality impacts, and AI Act transparency needs.
8. **OD draft stance:** Clearly separate value choices, empirical claims, and uncertain projections.
9. **Advisory poll candidate:** If useful, a non-binding poll question with neutral options and context.
10. **Human decision gate:** Required reviewer/body, evidence artifact, and approval threshold before publication as a position.

## GitHub issue labels to use

- `policy-backlog` — all items derived from this document.
- `evidence-needed` — item lacks a sufficient source map.
- `human-decision-gate` — cannot become a position without accountable human approval.
- `gdpr-ai-act-review` — has personal-data, profiling, AI transparency, or safety-report implications.
- `advisory-poll-candidate` — may be suitable for a non-binding MVP poll after review.
- `public-transparency` — should be mirrored on the public site or correction/audit log once reviewed.
