Current data posture

The public website uses necessary local storage for language and cookie-notice preferences. The lab must not collect political profiles, voting preferences or research participation data until a DPIA and explicit consent flow are in place.

  • Data minimisation is the default.
  • Participation consent and research consent must be separated.
  • Retention periods must be short, explicit and reviewable.
  • No microtargeted political persuasion based on sensitive data is allowed.

Your rights

For access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or other GDPR questions, contact admin@oberoendedigital.se. You may also contact the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY) if you believe your data rights have not been respected.
